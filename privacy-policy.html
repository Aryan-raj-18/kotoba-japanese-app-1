Kotoba Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · Developer: Aryan Raj · Contact: Aryanrajr868@gmail.com

Local learning data

Kotoba stores study progress, review schedules, mistakes, favorites, streaks, settings on your device. There is no signup or login screen. Local saving works with or without internet. Optional automatic cloud sync can be enabled only in a build connected to the publisher’s Firebase project. Previous local study records are preserved when upgrading; you can select or delete them in Data controls. The app has no advertising or analytics SDK.

Optional cloud sync

When cloud sync is available and you enable it, Kotoba sends a copy of your study progress, review history, favorites, streaks, roadmap and selected preferences to the publisher’s Google Firebase project using an anonymous cloud identity. Offline changes stay on the device and retry while the app is running. Cloud credentials stay in private app storage and are excluded from ordinary progress backups. Wallpapers, recordings, old passwords and reminder settings are not included in the cloud progress copy. Another installation starts with a separate identity. A private cloud key connects your own devices to the same cloud progress; ordinary Backup & restore also transfers progress. Keep the cloud key private because it grants access to that progress. Turning sync off stops future transfers but does not erase an existing cloud copy. Delete cloud copy in Internet & cloud sync removes the selected cloud study data and turns sync off once deletion succeeds; a queued deletion needs internet and the app running. Until Firebase is configured, this build sends no progress to a cloud service.

Microphone and speech

Microphone access is optional and requested only when you choose speaking or voice search. Recording fallback is temporary and stays in the app; Kotoba does not upload recordings to its developer. Android speech recognition is provided by your installed system speech service, which may send audio to its own servers. Its privacy practices and offline language-pack availability depend on your device. Japanese playback uses the installed text-to-speech service, which may also use a network voice. You can decline microphone permission and study using text or listening.

Photos, files and backups

The system picker lets you select a wallpaper or backup. The app does not scan your photo library. A wallpaper is stored locally. Exported backups contain learning history and settings, and are saved to the location you choose, including a cloud drive if you select one. Keep backups private. Import validates learning data before replacing current progress.

Notifications

Study reminders are optional. Notification permission and your chosen reminder time control them. You can disable reminders in settings or Android settings.

Deletion and retention

Data remains until you reset your learning progress, delete a previous study record, clear Android app storage, or uninstall the app. Data controls are available in the app menu. Deleting a previous study record removes its saved learning records and old credentials from this device. Exported backups and files you saved elsewhere must be deleted separately. Resetting browser settings removes the wallpaper and preferences.

Feedback and external links

Feedback drafted in the app is stored locally. Choosing an email link opens your email application; the message and contact information you send are received by the developer and used to respond. External websites and system services follow their own policies. Contact the developer to request deletion of feedback you have emailed.

Children and changes

Kotoba is a general language study app. It does not request a child’s personal information or operate a social feed. The publisher’s Play Store audience declaration must match the intended audience. Updated policies will be provided with future app releases.

# Speaking & Shadowing: original situation sentence set

`data/speaking_phrasebook.json` now contains **360 independently authored sentences** covering 24 practical situations, from daily routines, work and public transport to shopping, health and safety. Every entry has Japanese, a pronunciation reading, romaji, an English meaning, category and difficulty level. The earlier 177 PDF-selected phrases were removed and replaced, not just reworded.

The user-supplied *Essential Japanese: Speak Japanese With Confidence* PDF was consulted only for the **broad educational idea** of teaching language in practical situations. The new sentences, settings, combinations and examples were created independently; no book page mapping is present and the book’s sentences are not imported. The full source is kept in `build_original_speaking.py` for future content review and maintenance.

**Copyright:** Original examples lessen concerns about copying the book’s expression, but changing words alone is not a legal guarantee. Other content in the app has its own provenance and permissions, described in `CONTENT_LICENCES.md`.

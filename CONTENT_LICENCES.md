# Content and third-party notices

The full learning collection was restored from the publisher-supplied previous Kotoba project at the publisher's request. It includes reference-derived vocabulary examples and practice material alongside independently curated linguistic facts and generated study guidance. The restored material retains its source metadata where provided; this notice does not grant a new licence to third-party text or claim all of it is original. The publisher must verify distribution permissions for reference-derived material before public release.

The dedicated Speaking & Shadowing everyday-situation set is **independently authored** (360 new sentences in 24 situations); it replaces the earlier 177 source-selected phrasebook examples, which are no longer included in the set. The user-provided **Essential Japanese: Speak Japanese With Confidence** was used for high-level inspiration about practical language situations only, not as a source of sentences or book-page attribution. See `SPEAKING_PHRASEBOOK_SOURCE.md` and `build_original_speaking.py`. Review the remaining app datasets separately for rights clearance.

The literal word-learning examples generated by everyday_nl.py and the current app interface are original study guidance. Kana sounds, kanji readings/meanings and radical classifications are linguistic facts. Verbs are curated and conjugated by gen_verbs.py. Level labels are approximate study bands; the app is not affiliated with the JLPT.

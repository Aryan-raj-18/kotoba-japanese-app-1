On your phone

The notification shows in the notification shade and on the lock screen when Android allows it. Tap it to open that kanji, or use Next and Stop without opening Kotoba.

The kanji also becomes the notification icon. Some always-on displays show this icon; full card text and its position beneath the time/date depend on your phone. Kotoba cannot change the system clock or force an always-on display layout.

Show the card below the clock and weather
  1. Tap Show my kanji and allow notifications.
  2. Open Android notification settings below. Allow the Kanji study channel and choose to show its content on the lock screen.
  3. Open Phone settings below, search for “lock screen notifications,” and choose full notifications or cards if your phone offers that option. Some phones also require allowing silent notifications.
  4. For an always-on display, enable notification icons in your phone’s display settings.

Kotoba asks Android to show the full card. Android controls its location relative to the clock and weather; Kotoba cannot pin it directly below “27° cloudy.” An always-on display may show only the kanji icon.

Enable lock screen notifications and notification icons in your phone’s always-on display settings. Everything in this feature is saved on this device and works offline.

Automatic cloud sync

When available and enabled, cloud sync saves a second copy of your progress when internet returns. Local saving continues while disconnected.

Sync my progress

Checking cloud availability. Your progress stays on this device.

This device and the cloud have different progress. Keep a backup before choosing a copy. Other devices will receive the copy you choose.

Cloud sync covers study progress and selected preferences. Wallpaper, microphone recordings, reminders and private connection credentials are not uploaded in the progress copy.

No signup or login screen is required. Each installation starts with a separate private cloud identity. Connect your own devices with a private cloud key to sync the same progress.

Connect your devices

Save your private cloud key and keep it somewhere safe. It gives access to your cloud progress, so share it only with your own devices. A normal progress backup does not include this key.

To continue on another device or after reinstalling, first export that device's local progress. Choose your cloud key below and connect. Its cloud progress will replace the selected local study data; the previous copy is also kept as a recovery backup.
